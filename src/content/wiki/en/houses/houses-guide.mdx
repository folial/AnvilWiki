## What Are Great Houses in Exark?

Great Houses are the factions you can lead in Exark. Each House has its own unique ark, starting buildings, and specialized crewmates. Your House choice changes how you should build, hire, and plan your voyage.

## Great House Comparison

| House | Ark | Signature | Best For |
|-------|-----|-----------|----------|
| **House Arioch** | Diurnal Ark | Engineers & Artisans | Beginners - easier production loop |
| **House Mnesis** | Shepherd Moon | Precious raw materials | Players who want resource abundance |

> **Beginner recommendation:** Start with **House Arioch**. Its engineer and artisan focus gives you a smoother production loop.
