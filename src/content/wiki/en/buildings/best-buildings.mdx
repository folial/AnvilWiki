## The Core Rule: Food and Production First

Exark has over 100 buildings, but you should build them in a safe order.

| Priority | Building Type | Why It Matters |
|----------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Food buildings** | Food fuels buildings; stockpiles increase cycle speed |
| 2 | **Production buildings** | Production lets you construct more buildings |
| 3 | **Crew buildings** | Inspiration enables hiring crew with unique skills |
| 4 | **Gold buildings** | Gold buys buildings and crew at markets |
