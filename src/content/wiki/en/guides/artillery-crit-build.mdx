## What Is the Artillery Crit Build in Exark?

The Artillery Crit build uses cannons as your primary damage output, stacking crit rate and attack speed to melt enemies before they break your economy.

## Core Stats

| Stat | Target | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Crit rate** | 70%+ (can exceed 100%) | Turns every attack into massive damage |
| **Attack speed** | As high as possible | Triggers War Cry more often |
| **Armor / HP** | Keep balanced | Survive while ramping |

## How It Works

1. Build Artillery as your core damage source.
2. Stack crit rate very high, ideally above 70%.
3. Pair high attack speed with War Cry buildings for frequent full-map strikes.
4. Carry armor and HP bonuses to survive.

## Enemy Mechanics

| Enemy Ability | Counter |
|---------------|---------|
| Negative debuffs | Prioritize speed and purge effects |
| Summon copies when killed | Use AoE and sustained fire |
| Freeze or slow buildings | Disable or out-range them |
