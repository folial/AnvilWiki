## What Is the Guild Economy Build in Exark?

The Guild build is a powerful economy strategy. By building many buildings with the Guild trait, you can stack income bonuses exponentially. It is one of the fastest ways to generate huge amounts of Gold.

## Core Logic

- Each Guild building provides a bonus to other units with crafting traits.
- Every Guild building you own stacks another income effect.
- Even if a building's base income is low, stacking many creates a massive return.

## How to Build It

### Phase 1: Prepare Food

Before stacking Guild buildings, secure your Food supply. Without Food, your economy collapses before the build pays off.

### Phase 2: Stack Guild Buildings

Build as many Guild-related buildings as possible, such as Guild Halls, Farms, or Workshops with Guild traits.

### Phase 3: Convert Gold to Power

Use the huge Gold income to buy advanced buildings and relics.

### Phase 4: Maintain Defense

The build is expensive early. If enemies break your chain before it ramps, you can wipe.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Huge income potential | Needs strong early investment |
| Boosts Food, Industry, Academic | Vulnerable to early enemy pressure |
| Synergizes with many playstyles | If Gold chain breaks, run collapses |
