## What Is Exark?

Exark is a run-based engine builder and auto-battler where you command a gigantic ark-ship. Your goal is to lead your people to the rim of the world, away from a corrupted sun. Along the way you construct buildings, hire crew with unique skills, and exploit powerful synergies to keep your ark alive.

Unlike a traditional city builder, Exark is **run-based** - each voyage is a new run with new choices, similar to a roguelite.

## How to Start Your First Run

### 1. Pick Your Great House First

Your first decision is which **Great House** to lead. Each has its own ark, buildings, and crewmates, and plays differently.

| House | Ark | Signature | Best For |
|-------|-----|-----------|----------|
| **House Arioch** | Diurnal Ark | Engineers & Artisans | Beginners - easier production loop |
| **House Mnesis** | Shepherd Moon | Precious raw materials | Players who want resource abundance |

> **Beginner tip:** Start with **House Arioch**. Its engineer/artisan focus gives you a smoother production loop, which is the safest way to learn Exark's core systems.

### 2. Understand the Core Resources

Your ark runs on four resources:

| Resource | What It Does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Food** | Fuels your buildings; stockpiles increase cycle speed |
| **Production** | Lets you construct more buildings on the ark |
| **Inspiration** | Enables hiring crew with unique skills |
| **Gold** | Buy buildings/crew outright at dockside markets |

> The core loop is: **Food keeps buildings running - Production builds more - Inspiration hires crew - Gold buys shortcuts.**

### 3. Build Food & Production First

On your first run, focus on getting a stable **Food** and **Production** loop going before expanding. A Food stockpile increases your buildings' cycle speed, which accelerates everything else.

### 4. Hire Crew to Boost Buildings

Crew members have unique skills that improve the buildings they're assigned to. Assign crew to your most important production buildings first.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- **Expanding too fast** - Building too much before your resource loop is stable will crash your ark.
- **Ignoring Food** - Food is the foundation; without a stockpile your buildings slow down.
- **Not using crew** - Crew bonuses compound over time, so always keep buildings staffed.
