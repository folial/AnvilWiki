## Why Does Building Placement Matter in Exark?

Exark's ark has a limited grid, and where you put buildings directly affects your economy and survival. A building's effect often depends on its exact coordinates on the grid, so smart placement is a core strategy.

## Movable vs Immovable Buildings

| Type | Rule | Risk |
|------|------|------|
| **Movable** | Can be picked up and moved | There is a cap on movable buildings |
| **Immovable** | Once placed, cannot be removed | Powerful but inflexible; bad placement can lock you in |

### Movable Building Cap

Your ark has a limit on the number of movable buildings. Once you reach it, you must clear old buildings before placing new ones.

### Be Careful With Immovable Buildings

Some special buildings (often global-buff relic buildings) are immovable once placed. Think twice before placing them.

## Neighbor Effects

Many buildings are affected by what is next to them. An industrial building may only activate near a farming building, and some towers deal more damage near your core.

## Chain Reactions From Packing

Packing same-type buildings (like Guilds) can trigger global bonuses. Place high-value buildings in other buildings' ranges to maximize bonuses.

## Risk vs Reward: Ranging Too Far

Moving far gives more rewards, but enemies attack you longer. Their debuffs slow your loop, which can break your economy and collapse morale.
