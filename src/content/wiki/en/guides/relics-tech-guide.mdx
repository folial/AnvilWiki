## How Do Relics Work in Exark?

Relics are powerful permanent enhancements, like equipment slots for your ark. You can carry up to 10 Relics at once, so every pickup requires a decision.

## Relic Basics

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Cap | Up to 10 Relics |
| Most bonuses | Global, such as academic output or cycle speed |
| Some special effects | Require specific layouts or actions |

### Special Relic Tricks

Some Relics upgrade a building when consumed there, or gain stacking layers on each kill. Save space for Relics that fit your build.

## How Does Tech Work?

| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Academic points** | Unlock advanced buildings and blueprints |
| **Industrial points** | Improve production and economy |

### Balancing Academic and Industrial

- Need money? Invest in industrial early.
- Need advanced buildings? Invest in academic.

### Blueprint Unlocks

Some blueprints unlock by meeting special conditions, such as filling a meter on a creature building. Do not ignore production while focusing on combat.
