## The Four Core Resources

Exark runs on four resources:

| Resource | What It Does | Priority |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| **Food** | Fuels buildings; stockpiles increase cycle speed | 1 - Foundation |
| **Production** | Lets you construct more buildings | 2 - Expansion |
| **Inspiration** | Enables hiring crew with unique skills | 3 - Optimization |
| **Gold** | Buy buildings and crew outright at dockside markets | 4 - Shortcut |

## The Core Loop

Food keeps buildings running, then Production builds more, then Inspiration hires crew, then Gold buys shortcuts.

## How to Balance Resources

1. Start with Food - without a Food stockpile, your buildings slow down.
2. Add Production - once Food is stable, expand your ark.
3. Invest in Inspiration - hire crew to boost your best buildings.
4. Use Gold when you have surplus - do not rely on Gold for your core economy.
