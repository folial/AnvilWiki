## 10 Quick Tips for Exark Beginners

1. Pick House Arioch first - its engineer focus makes learning the game easier.
2. Build Food buildings before anything else - without Food, your ark stalls.
3. Keep a Food stockpile - stockpiles increase your buildings cycle speed.
4. Do not expand too fast - build one building at a time until your loop is stable.
5. Always staff buildings with crew - even a mismatched crew member is better than an empty slot.
6. Use Inspiration early - crew bonuses compound over the entire run.
7. Gold is a shortcut, not a strategy - prioritize Food and Production first.
8. Read your Great House bonuses - each House has unique strengths; build around them.
9. Pause and plan - Exark has pause and time acceleration; use them to think before building.
10. Experiment with different Houses - each House teaches a different playstyle.
