## How to Win in Exark

Exark gives you multiple ways to reach the rim of the world:

| Route | Strategy | Best For |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Peaceful Production** | Focus on resource output, building efficiency, and stable economy | Players who enjoy optimization |
| **Military Conquest** | Build combat-focused buildings and blast enemies into submission | Players who prefer direct confrontation |

## Choosing Your Route

Your Great House choice may influence which route is easier. House Arioch's engineer focus likely favors peaceful production, while other Houses may have combat advantages.

> Specific victory conditions, required buildings, and turn counts for each route are still being verified from the 1.0 release.
